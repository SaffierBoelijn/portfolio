About me

I'm Saffier Boelijn, an ICT student. Frontend web development is what I'm best at right now, and I'm going back over the basics of HTML and CSS on the side.

In 2021 I watched my brother code and wanted to build a Discord bot myself. After that I picked it up now and then, when I had time or needed something that didn't exist yet. I had ICT in my last two years of school, and that's when I really kept going.

I like solving problems and building things that actually get used. That's why every project below has something in it I can't do yet.

This website too. I built it by hand, without a framework, so I can explain every line.
